## Summary

Kovacevich's pro-corporate argument has **6 core pillars**:

1. **Anti-corporate populism is electorally toxic** — Biden adopted it and lost working class votes
2. **Democrats should be the party of business** — Republicans abandoned business, Dems should fill the void
3. **Big Tech antitrust was a political waste** — netted nothing electorally while alienating donors and voters
4. **Corporate success funds social programs** — Big Tech profits = California schools and safety net
5. **Anti-corporate populism is substantively wrong** — housing, energy, and child care aren't caused by "corporate power"
6. **Regulation entrenches incumbents** — overregulation hurts the startups and consumers Dems claim to help
