## Summary

Yglesias's consistent argument across ~3 years: pro-Israel positions are electorally safer for Democrats because:
1. **Public opinion favors Israel** — more voters want Democrats *more* pro-Israel than less
2. **The median voter is older and pro-Israel** — youth opinion is exaggerated
3. **Gaza is low-salience for swing voters** — they care about inflation, healthcare, abortion
4. **Pro-Israel money is reliable** — it's the only consistent source of moderate/corporate fundraising
5. **The issue divides Democrats and unites Republicans** — salience helps the GOP
6. **Moving left on Israel costs votes on the right/center without gaining credit on the left**

He seldom says he's personally pro-Israel on the merits; rather, his argument is consistently about political strategy — that Democrats hurt themselves by catering to the anti-Israel wing.
