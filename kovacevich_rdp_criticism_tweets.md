# Adam Kovacevich: Revolving Door Project Criticism Tweets

Kovacevich (founder of Chamber of Progress, a tech industry trade group) repeatedly criticizes the Revolving Door Project (@revolvingdoorDC) and its executive director Jeff Hauser (@jeffhauser). His main lines of attack:

1. **RDP is inconsistent** — they oppose revolving door hires when it suits them but ignore cases involving allies (e.g., Kanter, who represented Yelp/Microsoft/News Corp)
2. **RDP undermines Democratic electoral success** — purity tests shrink the coalition and cost Dems elections
3. **RDP ignores Trump's revolving door** — while obsessing over Biden/Harris hires, they ignore Trump's lobbyist-filled cabinet
4. **RDP is not invested in Democrats winning** — they prefer Dems in the minority so they can enforce purity tests
5. **RDP lacks funding transparency** — while attacking others' corporate ties
